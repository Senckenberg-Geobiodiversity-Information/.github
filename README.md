# Geobiodiversity Information and Data Management, Senckenberg Data and Modelling Centre (SDMC)
# FAIR data, research infrastructures, metadata and collection digitisation, (marine) biodiversity informatics, and open science 

# About Our Team 
We lead Senckenberg-wide Geobiodiversity Information and Data Management, advancing FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable and Reusable) data practices, research data management, collection data curation, and biodiversity informatics. We develop and maintain data infrastructures, standards, and workflows that connect Senckenberg's scientific collections and research data with national and international biodiversity information systems. Our work combines data management, collection digitisation, biodiversity informatics, ecological modelling, and open science to make geobiodiversity data accessible, interoperable, and reusable for research, conservation, and evidence-based decision-making.

# Research Data Management & Data Systems
Develop, manage, and maintain Senckenberg data management systems, the Senckenberg (meta)data portal, and associated workflows.
Support data archiving, standardisation, quality assurance, publication, integration, and reuse.
Coordinate annual research data management training and promote FAIR data practices.
Support responsible data governance, documentation, provenance, and sustainable data stewardship.

# Collection Management Services
Collaborate with the Digital Collection Infrastructures working group on collection management systems, including SeSam and the future DINA infrastructure.
Contribute to data standardisation, quality control, digitisation, data mobilisation, and training.
Facilitate the publication and integration of collection data into international open-access repositories.

# National & International Research Infrastructures 
We contribute to and coordinate major biodiversity and research data infrastructures:
NFDI4Biodiversity – Mobilising biodiversity and environmental data for collective use.
Senckenberg GFBio Data Centre – Supporting the curation, archiving, and publication of natural science research data.
GBIF Germany – Invertebrates III Node – Coordinating biodiversity data mobilisation and exchange for invertebrates.
OBIS Deep-Sea Node – Managing and mobilising deep-sea biodiversity occurrence data through the Ocean Biodiversity Information System.
UNESCO-IOC IODE Associate Data Unit – Supporting the standardisation and international exchange of marine biodiversity data.

# Additional Tasks
Through collaboration with CETAF, GBIF, OBIS, LifeWatch ERIC, Marine Biodiversity Monitoring for Europe (MBON), and the wider biodiversity informatics community, we strengthen interoperability between taxonomic knowledge, scientific collections, and biodiversity data infrastructures.
We also coordinate the Senckenberg IT-User Board, fostering collaboration and alignment between IT users, scientific IT, and Central IT.

# Research
Our research transforms heterogeneous biodiversity information into actionable knowledge for science, conservation, and informed decision-making.
We integrate data from scientific literature, natural history collections, biodiversity databases, and monitoring programmes to investigate:
Biodiversity patterns and drivers across geographic regions, environmental gradients, and depth ranges.
Data and knowledge gaps, including biases in biodiversity observations and their implications for ecological inference and conservation.
Marine and global biodiversity, using taxonomy, macroecology, biogeography, and ecological modelling.
Biodiversity informatics and open data, developing reproducible workflows for data mobilisation, quality assessment, integration, and reuse.
Biodiversity monitoring and science–policy interfaces, supporting evidence-based assessments, including the work of IPBES.

Our methodological approaches combine geobiodiversity informatics, statistical and ecological modelling, spatial analysis, and data-driven methods to improve the accessibility, quality, and analytical value of biodiversity information and transfer this knowledge for informed decision-making processes and actionable plans.
